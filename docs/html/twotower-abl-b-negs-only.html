Adding a second negative made things worse

Read against Arm C, the baseline corner

Against the baseline corner (Arm C), adding a second negative per row with no other change lost 0.040 pair AUC and 0.035 recall@1. The likely cause is in the data: only 189 of 385 seekers-queries have two genuinely different rejected candidates, so 27.5% of the second-negative slots are duplicates of the first. A repeated negative adds no information but doubles that example's weight in the loss.
