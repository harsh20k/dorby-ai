Does It Matter What Text the Model Trains On?

twotower_query_weighted/ found that swapping the fine-tuned model's seeker text at eval time — profile-only, query-only, or a blend, with zero retraining — moves recall@1 a lot (0.13 to 0.32). Two follow-up experiments each actually trained a fresh adapter on one of those representations from scratch, then scored it on text matching its own training. Both land within noise of just swapping text on the standard (concat-trained) model: no_query_001 (trained profile-only) vs. the profile-only swap, and query_only_001 (trained query-only) vs. the query-only swap. Training condition barely matters — what's fed in at eval time is what moves the numbers, for both representations. (One bug caught along the way, see below.)

Bug: the first eval didn't match training

eval_real_full.eval.run_eval always builds seeker text as profile+query — correct for every other adapter in this project, wrong for no_query_001, which trained on profile-only text. Caught before this was published. Full writeup: docs/possible-bugs.md #5.
