Reading

Frozen nano already has the retrieval on packing 1 (R@1 0.30 / R@10 0.91). Fine-tuning barely moves R@K. The free top1_ctrl query-only swap is still the best retrieval row (R@1 0.32, MRR 0.508, R@5 0.80).

Hard-neg AUC is the training lever. Packing 2 on frozen nano is below chance (0.437). Training on that trim jumps it to 0.656 — best of the four, and the reason queryonly_back_look_001 exists. Trimming the candidate at eval without training that way hurts hard-negs.
