What's different this time

The earlier version of this page (holdout-embedding-space-3d.html) split a person’s lookingFor field into separate asks, but kept every other field of their profile in the text each time. The one thing that changed barely moved the point, because everything unchanged still dominated the vector.

This page does the opposite: every dot here comes from exactly one field’s text and nothing else. A person’s positioning alone, their notes alone, one lookingFor ask alone — no shared context left to hold anything in place.

Isolating a field moves it a lot more

A lone field scores similar to that same person’s whole-profile point — far looser than the earlier experiment’s near-identical 0.89–0.90. Two different people’s whole profiles still score , so a lone field is now much closer to the boundary between “still recognizably this person” and “just floating on its own topic.” A person’s own fields spread out to about of the gap between people — noticeably wider than the earlier constellation.

Do same-named fields cluster by topic, or by person?

A different question this isolation makes possible: take everyone’s notes field alone, ignore whose it is, and check how similar they are to each other — then compare that to how similar each one is to its own owner’s whole profile. If same-named fields cluster together regardless of person, embedding a field “alone” risks losing whose profile it belongs to.

When the right column is close to the middle one, that field’s text reads as generic across people — the model can barely tell whose it is once it’s alone. When the middle column is clearly higher, the field still carries person-specific signal even in isolation.

Every point on this page comes from one field’s text only, no search query, no other field. Similarity is measured on the full 1,024-number vectors, not the flattened 3D view. Vectors are read out of a local Chroma collection (artifacts/voyage_nano_field_isolation/chroma), built by scripts/load_field_isolation_to_chroma.py from embeddings computed on Modal by baselines/voyage_nano_field_isolation/modal_embed_space.py.
